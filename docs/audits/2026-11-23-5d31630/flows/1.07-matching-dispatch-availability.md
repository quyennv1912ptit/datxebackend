## 1. End-to-End Dispatch & Driver Availability Flow

## 5. New Confirmed Finding (Task 1.07)

### `F-004` — `AdminDriverService#forceOffline` Allows Forcing a `BUSY` Driver Offline and Clears `onlineVehicleType`, Permanently Bricking `START_TRIP`
- **Category**: State Integrity / Driver Availability & Active Ride Coupling
- **Task ID**: `1.07` | **SHA**: `5d316306e7fe07514e295b6b5fcb5ff9725c257a`
- **Location**: `src/main/kotlin/com/nhom15/datxebackend/admin/service/AdminDriverService.kt:72-89`; coupled with `RideDispatchRecoveryTask.kt:72-78` and `RideStateMachine.kt:105-110` (`DriverVerificationEligibility.kt:63-65`)
- **Actor / Entrypoint**: `ADMIN` via `POST /api/admin/drivers/{id}/force-offline` (`AdminDriverController#forceOffline`)
- **Preconditions**: Driver `7` is `BUSY` on an active Ride `42` in `DRIVER_ASSIGNED`, `DRIVER_ARRIVING`, `DRIVER_ARRIVED`, or `VEHICLE_HANDOVER` (`driver.onlineVehicleType = CAR`).
- **Expected Behavior** *(source: `DriverPresenceService.kt:79-83` & `DriverVerificationEligibility.kt:63-65`)*: A driver with an active ride (`BUSY`) or pending offer cannot be forced offline with `onlineVehicleType = null` while the ride is in progress (or must be rejected with `409 DRIVER_NOT_AVAILABLE`), and `AuthenticatedUserCache` must be evicted on status changes.
- **Actual Behavior**:
  1. `AdminDriverService#forceOffline` (`AdminDriverService.kt:75-78`) acquires `findByIdForUpdate(driverId)` and **unconditionally** sets `driver.status = DriverStatus.OFFLINE` and `driver.onlineVehicleType = null` — with zero check for `DriverStatus.BUSY`, active rides, pending offers, or `DriverStatus.BLOCKED`.
  2. `AdminDriverService` does not inject or evict `AuthenticatedUserCache`.
  3. `afterCommit` calls `locationService.removeDriver(7)` → deletes `driver:alive:7` and sets Redis `driver:7:status = "OFFLINE"` (while `driver:7:current_ride = "42"` remains).
  4. Within 10s, `RideDispatchRecoveryTask` (`RideDispatchRecoveryTask.kt:72-78`) restores `driver.status = DriverStatus.BUSY` in DB and Redis, **but NEVER restores `driver.onlineVehicleType` (remains `null`)**.
  5. Driver `7` cannot call `PUT /api/driver/availability` (`online=true`) to re-set `onlineVehicleType` because `DriverPresenceService.kt:63` rejects `BUSY` / `hasActiveRide` with `409 DRIVER_NOT_AVAILABLE`.
  6. When Driver `7` calls `POST /api/driver/rides/42/start`, `RideStateMachine.kt:108` calls `verification.mayDriveForRide(driver, ride, starting = true)` → `DriverVerificationEligibility.kt:63` checks `driver.onlineVehicleType != ride.vehicleType` (`null != CAR`) → returns `false` → throws **`409 DRIVER_NOT_VERIFIED` permanently**, bricking the active ride until cancellation!
- **Reproduction (Fake Data)**:
  1. Driver `7` (`onlineVehicleType = CAR`) accepts Ride `42` → `DRIVER_ASSIGNED`, completes pickup handover → `VEHICLE_HANDOVER`.
  2. As `ADMIN`, call `POST /api/admin/drivers/7/force-offline` → returns `200 OK`; DB now has `drivers(7).status = OFFLINE, online_vehicle_type = NULL`.
  3. Driver `7` calls `POST /api/driver/rides/42/start` → fails with `409 DRIVER_NOT_VERIFIED` (both immediately and after `RideDispatchRecoveryTask` restores `status = BUSY`).
  4. Driver `7` attempts `PUT /api/driver/availability` (`online=true, vehicle_type=CAR`) → fails with `409 DRIVER_NOT_AVAILABLE`.
- **Root Cause**: `AdminDriverService#forceOffline` omits the active-ride/BUSY/pending-offer/BLOCKED guard present in `DriverPresenceService#updateAvailability`, clears `onlineVehicleType = null` on a driver with an active ride, and omits `AuthenticatedUserCache` eviction.
- **Impact / Blast Radius**: Any active pre-start ride whose driver is accidentally or concurrently force-offlined by an admin is permanently stuck and cannot transition to `IN_PROGRESS`. Also allows `AdminRideService#assignDriver` to falsely treat the connected driver as disconnected (since `driver:alive` was deleted).
- **Confidence**: High | **Verdict**: `CONFIRMED` (**source-confirmed**) | **Severity**: **`P1` High**
- **Minimal Fix**: In `AdminDriverService#forceOffline`, check `if (driver.status == DriverStatus.BUSY || rideRepository.existsByDriverIdAndStatusIn(driverId, ACTIVE_RIDE_STATUSES) || offers.driverHasOffer(driverId)) throw ConflictException(ErrorCode.DRIVER_NOT_AVAILABLE)` (and do not downgrade `DriverStatus.BLOCKED` to `OFFLINE`), and evict `AuthenticatedUserCache` after commit. Also harden `RideDispatchRecoveryTask.kt:73` to restore `onlineVehicleType = ride.vehicleType` if null on an active ride.
- **Proposed Regression Test**: Unit/integration test asserting `AdminDriverService#forceOffline` throws `ConflictException(DRIVER_NOT_AVAILABLE)` when driver is `BUSY` or has an active ride/offer, leaves `BLOCKED` intact, and evicts `AuthenticatedUserCache` when forcing an `ONLINE` driver offline.
