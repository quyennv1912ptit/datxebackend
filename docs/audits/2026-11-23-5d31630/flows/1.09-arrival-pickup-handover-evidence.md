## 3. Evidence Integrity, Ownership & UI-Contract Verification

- **No Step Skipping**: `RideStatusTransitions` strictly enforces linear progression `DRIVER_ASSIGNED → DRIVER_ARRIVING → DRIVER_ARRIVED → VEHICLE_HANDOVER → IN_PROGRESS`. `START_TRIP` fails with `400 HANDOVER_REPORT_REQUIRED` if pickup report is missing or `confirmedByCustomer == false`, and `409 INVALID_RIDE_STATE` if called before `VEHICLE_HANDOVER`.
- **Cloudinary Ownership & Signature (`UploadSignatureService.kt:37-47`, `CloudinaryAssetService.kt:38-81`)**:
  - Handover signature requires `Role.DRIVER`, ride participation (`requireParticipant`), and open ride (`requireOpen`), pinning `folder = datxe/handover/{rideId}`.
  - `validateHandoverImage` verifies `publicId` starts with `datxe/handover/{rideId}/`, rejects `.`/`..`/`\`, verifies `https://res.cloudinary.com/{cloudName}/image/upload/v{n}/{publicId}.{ext}` with no query/fragment — blocking cross-ride evidence reuse (`rideId` mismatch) and external Cloudinary clouds (contrast with `F-028` in `TicketService`).
  - Customer correction images are validated via `validateCustomerImage` (`app_dat_xe/customer/{id}/`).
- **Immutability After Confirmation**: Both `HandoverCorrectionService#request` (`L35`) and `revise` (`L52`) reject any edit once `confirmedByCustomer == true` (`409 Conflict`). Historical photos replaced during `revise` remain preserved in `handover_revisions.snapshot_json` (intentionally not deleted from Cloudinary so revision history stays viewable).
- **Hardening Notes (Non-Blocking / Low Risk)**:
  1. `RideHandoverService#createPickup` (`L56`) and `createDropoff` (`L118`) read `rides` via unlocked `findById` before inserting `report`/`photos` and only acquire `rides FOR UPDATE` later inside `stateMachine.transition` (`L94`/`L165`). Because the entire method is `@Transactional`, a concurrent cancel rolls back `report`/`photos` cleanly (no orphan DB rows), though locking `rides FOR UPDATE` upfront (as in `HandoverCorrectionService.kt:80`) avoids wasted inserts and lock-order asymmetry.
  2. `validateHandoverImage` scopes to `datxe/handover/{rideId}/` without separating `pickup/` vs `dropoff/`, so a driver could technically submit the same `rideId` pickup URLs in dropoff (mitigated by mandatory customer inspection & confirmation before `COMPLETE`).
  3. `confirmPickup` only allows `VEHICLE_HANDOVER` (`L193`), whereas `confirmDropoff` allows `VEHICLE_RETURNED` and `COMPLETED` (`L229`) for idempotent retry after completion.
